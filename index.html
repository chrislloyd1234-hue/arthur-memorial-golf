In Loving Memory

"The game of golf is a search for truth, companionship, and the perfect pint at the 19th hole."

The Arthur Hothersall Memorial Golfing Challenge was established in 2007 as an annual tribute to a wonderful man whose love for the fairway was only surpassed by his warmth, generosity, and laughter.

Every year, eight players assemble to compete for the prestigious Memorial Trophy. Though only one player’s name is engraved on the silver shield each autumn, the real victory lies in the bonds of family, the shared history of wild hooks and spectacular putts, and the memories forged over 36 holes.
